## Options

### Colors

- **Default** – respects system's light/dark preference
- **Hotdog Stand** – classic win95 hotdog stand color scheme
- **VGUI** – valve's VGUI color scheme
- **Gruvbox** – dark, warm palette (default recommended)
- **Windows 2000** – win2k-inspired look

### Reduced game list entry height

Makes the game list entries' height smaller.
